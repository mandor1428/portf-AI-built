Challenge → Outcome

Challenge

Turn fragmented MLS listings, public records and market signals into a single trustworthy decision surface — scored, ranked and explorable state-by-state on an interactive intelligence grid.

Approach

Built a unified data layer over MLS + public records, an LLM-driven analysis pipeline (value, condition, risks, ROI) and a pro dashboard with intelligence grid, Analyze Property, Compare, Investment Analysis, Cost Calculator, Neighborhood Intel, Market Insights and an AI Chat assistant.

Stack

TypeScript · React · Node.js · Python · LLM Orchestration · PostgreSQL · MLS & Public Records APIs

Outcome

Live at pro.houspector.com with 471k+ properties analyzed, 45k+ strong-buy signals and an 83.3 average AI score powering daily decisions for home buyers and investors.

What I built

Highlights

  • Houspector Intelligence Grid — interactive U.S. heatmap toggling AI Score, Properties Analyzed, Strong Buys and Average ROI per state.
  • Conversational AI assistant guiding non-expert buyers through search, comparison and investment decisions.
  • Automated property analysis pipeline scoring value, condition, risks and investment potential in seconds.
  • Full pro suite: Analyze Property, Compare, Investment Analysis, Cost Calculator, Neighborhood Intel, Market Insights and Child-Friendly scoring.
