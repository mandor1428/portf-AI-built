About this service

Applied AI Systems is my end-to-end practice for turning messy, real-world data into models that actually run in production.

I work with founders, research teams and operators who don't just want a notebook — they want a system that ships, scales and keeps learning.

Every engagement starts with the boring but critical work: defining the prediction target, auditing the data, and writing an evaluation harness that mirrors the way the model will be judged in the wild.

From there I design training pipelines in PyTorch with mixed-precision, gradient checkpointing and reproducible seeding, so runs can be compared honestly across weeks of iteration.

I obsess over evaluation more than over architectures — stratified splits, demographic cohorts, and regression gates that catch silent quality drops before a deploy.

On the infrastructure side, models are packaged as FastAPI or Triton services with proper timeouts, retries and structured logging, not toy Flask apps.

Inference is profiled and tuned for sub-200ms P95 latency on GPU and CPU, with ONNX or quantized fallbacks where it makes sense.

I've shipped systems that held #1 rank for 30+ days on Bittensor's medical imaging subnet, with 94%+ top-1 accuracy on held-out clinical data.

I've also built forecasting engines that cut MAE by more than 20% versus strong baselines on noisy financial time series.

Beyond the first launch, I set up continuous retraining, drift monitoring and a model registry, so the system improves instead of decaying.

I work in the open with your team — pairing on hard problems, documenting decisions, and leaving behind code your engineers actually want to maintain.

If you're between 'we have an idea' and 'we have a product', this is the engagement that gets you across.
